## Description

I think this gets done in two parts, for printability.
  1. The ordinary snap, but with 4 cutout pegs
  2. A feather board piece that sits in the pegs

A sheet of cardboard (3-4mm) will sit between two featherboards.
But arrange with symmetry so that it can be centered on the snap or between two adjacent snaps.
